# Author Notes

## Motivation

Since high school, I have been interested in the internal financial operations of businesses and the practical use of accounting and economics. I helped run a small family business from the age of 16, which allowed me to understand workflows ranging from ordering goods and accounting to revenue tracking and auditing.

## Why This Idea

I focused the application on self-employed professionals and small businesses that may not want to rely on large enterprise SaaS platforms such as SAP or Oracle.

## Inspiration

Inspired by the open source [IDURAR ERP CRM](https://github.com/idurar/idurar-erp-crm).

Vitesse shares the problem domain but was built independently:
- frontend rewritten in React + TypeScript with Vite

- backend extended with multi tenancy, RBAC, state machines, activity stream, and a full test suite — none of which are present in the original

## Next Goals

[OPERIS CORE](https://github.com/MikeMikeRx/operis-operations-core) is a backend focused SaaS system where I implement real world business logic and security in more depth.
