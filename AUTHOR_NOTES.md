# Author Notes

## Motivation

I chose CRM/ERP software as my first full-stack application to gain real-world experience with financial and business-oriented SaaS systems.

Since high school, I have been interested in the internal financial operations of businesses and the practical use of accounting and economics. I helped run a small family business from the age of 16, which allowed me to understand workflows ranging from ordering goods and accounting to revenue tracking and auditing.

## Why This Idea

I wanted to explore how CRM systems structure business workflows and what lies behind the user interface that makes them usable in real-world environments. I focused the application on self-employed professionals and small businesses that may not want to rely on large enterprise SaaS platforms such as SAP or Oracle.

## Inspiration

This project was inspired by the open-source repository [IDURAR ERP CRM](https://github.com/idurar/idurar-erp-crm). I kept the backend in JavaScript as a learning reference while rebuilding the frontend in TypeScript and adding my own features and dashboard design.

## Next Goals

I want to continue focusing on business-oriented software and explore modern approaches to SaaS development. My recent project, [OPERIS CORE](https://github.com/MikeMikeRx/operis-operations-core), is a backend-focused SaaS system where I explore real-world business logic and security in more depth.

## For Recruiters

This project reflects my transition from learning programming to building real business-oriented software systems.  
My goal is to continue growing as a developer by working on production systems and learning from experienced engineers.
